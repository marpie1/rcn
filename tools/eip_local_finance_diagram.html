EIP Stage Sketch: Local Finance

Exploring Causal Relations Across Ecological, Institutional, and Political Domains
STAGE LEFT
🌍 ECOLOGY
🌎 Global/Gaia
→ Finite planet constrains real wealth (energy, materials, biological productivity)
← Global financial flows drive extraction, Climate change, Inequality
To Finance: Planetary boundaries limit real economy; Climate risks threaten financial stability; Resource depletion affects values
From Finance: Speculation drives commodity booms; Debt forces extraction; Carbon markets commodify atmosphere; Financial crises triggered by ecological limits
🌊 Bioregion/Watershed
→ Regional productive capacity (soil, water, forests, fisheries) is real wealth
← Financial extraction depletes bioregional wealth without reinvestment
To Finance: Natural resources as collateral; Agricultural land values; Water rights; Tourism value
From Finance: Absentee ownership extracts profits without ecological care; Debt forces overexploitation; Financialization separates ownership from stewardship
🏞️ Local Ecosystem
→ Ecosystem services provide value (pollination, water filtration, climate regulation)
← Unpriced in markets; Financial decisions ignore ecological costs
To Finance: Ecosystem collapse affects property values, Agriculture, Insurance costs
From Finance: Short-term profit horizons ignore long-term ecological degradation; Externalized costs; "Discount rate" devalues future
🏡 Homestead/Property
→ Property as financial asset vs. home vs. productive land
← Mortgage debt, Property taxes, Speculation affect land use
To Finance: Property values determined by location, Improvements, Market speculation
From Finance: Mortgage debt shapes what people can afford; Property taxes force monetization; Speculation disconnects property from use; Foreclosure = displacement
Ecological Variables
Real Wealth vs. Financial Wealth
Real: Soil, Water, Energy, Skills, Tools; Financial: Numbers in accounts (can be created infinitely)
Discount Rate / Short-Termism
Financial logic devalues future; 7% discount = $100 in 50 years worth $3 today
Ecological Debt
Resource extraction, Pollution exported from Global North to South; Financial debt flows opposite direction
Bioregional Economic Resilience
Capacity to meet needs locally if global finance collapses
CENTER STAGE
🏛️ INSTITUTIONS & FINANCE
LOCAL FINANCE
(Primary Variable - Local Control Can Increase or Decrease)
Community Development Financial Institutions (CDFIs) / Credit Unions
Type: Commons/Member-owned
Affects: Local lending (+), Affordable credit (+), Community ownership (+), Wealth circulation (+)
Affected by: Federal CDFI Fund, State regulation, Member deposits, Mission drift risk
Ecology link: Can prioritize green lending, Local food systems, Renewable energy
Politics link: Federal CDFI certification; State credit union laws; Alternative to extractive banks
Local Currencies / Time Banks / Mutual Credit Systems
Type: Commons-based
Affects: Local economic circulation (+), Non-extractive exchange (+), Community building (+), Limited scale (-)
Affected by: Legal status, Community participation, Merchant acceptance, Tax treatment
Ecology link: Favor local exchange; Reduce long-distance trade; Can't extract compound interest from living world
Politics link: Municipal tolerance; Federal currency laws; Seen as threat by mainstream finance
Public Banks / Municipal Banks
Type: Public/Government-owned
Affects: Public ownership of finance (+), Local investment (+), Lower costs (+), Rare in US (-)
Affected by: State enabling legislation, Political will, Banking lobby opposition, Initial capitalization
Ecology link: Can prioritize green infrastructure, Local renewable energy, Sustainable projects
Politics link: State legislation required; Bank of North Dakota only US example; Massive bank lobbying against
Commercial Banks / Wall Street Banks
Type: EXTRACTIVE
Affects: Wealth extraction (-), Redlining (-), Fees/penalties (-), Credit denial (-), Speculation (-), Too big to fail (-)
Affected by: Federal Reserve, FDIC, Regulations (weak post-2008), Shareholder demands
Ecology link: Indifferent to ecological impact; Fund fossil fuels, Mining, Deforestation; Short-term profit only
Politics link: Federal regulation (captured); Campaign finance; Revolving door; 2008 bailouts; "Too big to fail"
Payday Lenders / Check Cashers / Predatory Finance
Type: EXTRACTIVE - Debt traps
Affects: Poverty deepening (-), Debt spirals (-), Desperation exploitation (-), Wealth extraction from poorest (-)
Affected by: State usury laws (often preempted), Federal CFPB (gutted), Desperation of unbanked
Ecology link: No ecological connection; Pure financial extraction
Politics link: State usury law fights; Industry lobbying; Targets communities of color; Financial apartheid
Crowdfunding / Community Investment
Type: Commons (when non-extractive) / Private (when equity-based)
Affects: Access to capital (+), Community ownership (+/-), Speculation risk (-), Democratization (+)
Affected by: SEC regulations, Platform fees, Community trust, Project quality
Ecology link: Can fund community solar, Local food, Green projects; Or anything
Politics link: JOBS Act opened equity crowdfunding; SEC oversight; Can bypass traditional gatekeepers
Municipal Bonds / Local Government Borrowing
Type: Public (but investors extract interest)
Affects: Infrastructure funding (+), Debt burden (-), Tax-free interest for wealthy (-), Future obligations (-)
Affected by: Credit ratings, Interest rates, State debt limits, Voter approval, Federal tax exemption
Ecology link: Can fund green infrastructure or highways/sprawl depending on politics
Politics link: Municipal/county financing; Rating agencies; Bondholders extract wealth; Detroit bankruptcy
Private Equity / Hedge Funds / Vulture Funds
Type: EXTRACTIVE - Maximum extraction
Affects: Asset stripping (-), Debt loading (-), Job cuts (-), Community destruction (-), Short-term profit (-)
Affected by: Securities regulation, Tax treatment, Access to cheap credit, Bankruptcy law shields
Ecology link: Indifferent or hostile; Buy nursing homes, Newspapers, Housing = extract maximum, Abandon
Politics link: Federal tax treatment; Buy distressed municipal bonds; Profit from austerity; Political capture
Revolving Loan Funds / Community Investment Funds
Type: Commons-based
Affects: Capital recycling (+), Mission-driven (+), Patient capital (+), Limited scale (-)
Affected by: Initial capital, Repayment rates, Administrative capacity, Community need
Ecology link: Can fund ecological projects with long payback periods
Politics link: Often seeded with federal/state grants; Neighborhood level; Alternative to banks
Key Institutional Variables
Local Wealth Circulation vs. Extraction
Does money circulate locally or leak out immediately?
Affected by: Local ownership, Banking structure, Import dependence, Chain stores vs. Local
Affects: Local prosperity, Multiplier effect, Community resilience, Tax base
Interest / Usury / Compound Growth
Interest = wealth transfer from borrowers to lenders; Compound growth impossible on finite planet
Affected by: Federal Reserve, Usury laws, Cultural norms, Religious prohibitions
Affects: Debt burdens, Inequality, Extraction pressure, Growth imperative
Financialization / Speculation
Everything becomes asset class for speculation (housing, Food, Water, Carbon)
Affected by: Deregulation, Technology, Wealth concentration, Global capital flows
Affects: Price volatility, Disconnection from use value, Extraction, Crises
Credit Access Inequality
Redlining, Credit scores, Collateral requirements exclude poor/communities of color
Affected by: Racism, Wealth inequality, Banking practices, Regulation
Affects: Business formation, Home ownership, Wealth building, Opportunity
Debt Burden / Debt Peonage
Student debt, Medical debt, Payday loans trap people in permanent debt
Affected by: Wage stagnation, Costs (education, Healthcare, Housing), Predatory lending, Bankruptcy law
Affects: Economic freedom, Risk-taking, Organizing capacity, Political power

Key Insights from This EIP Analysis

  • Money is Created by Banks Through Lending: 97% of money supply is created by private banks making loans, not government printing. Banks create money out of nothing when they lend, then collect interest. This is enormous power given to private corporations.
  • Interest Requires Perpetual Growth: Compound interest means debt grows exponentially. On finite planet, this is impossible. System requires continuous extraction to service debt. This is structural driver of ecological destruction.
  • Federal Reserve Serves Banks, Not Public: Fed is privately owned by member banks. "Federal" is misleading. Controls money supply and interest rates to manage inflation and bank stability, not public good. 2008 bailouts revealed whose interests matter.
  • Local Wealth Leakage is Massive: Chain stores, National banks, Absentee landlords extract wealth from communities. Money spent locally circulates 2-3x creating multiplier effect. Money sent to Wall Street or corporate HQ never returns.
  • Debt is Social Control: Student debt, Medical debt, Mortgage debt constrain life choices. Can't strike, Can't organize, Can't take risks, Can't leave bad jobs when you're servicing debt. This is political function of debt, not just economic.
  • Public Banks Would be Transformative: Bank of North Dakota partners with local banks, Keeps profits in-state, Funds infrastructure cheaply, Enables student loans at 2%. Banking lobby blocks replication elsewhere. Only one
