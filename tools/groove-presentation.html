The problem
Care coordination happens in the gaps
A Community Health Worker visits someone at home. They write notes on paper. Later they enter them into a system the person can't access. A family member heard something different. The doctor sees none of it.

The care team is a team in name only. They share no common record, no coordination space, and no way to know who said what or when — while the person their care plan belongs to controls none of it.
3 / 10
Who it is for
A care team of 2–4 people

Person of Record

The individual receiving care. Controls all access. Sees exactly who has viewed their plan. Nothing leaves the workspace without their knowledge.

Community Health Worker

Works alongside the person during home visits. Enters observations — always marked "Entered by other." Uses a phone during visits.

Family Member

1–2 people the person has invited. Can read pages and join discussions. Cannot edit the health record directly.

The whole unit

Shared view of the health record. A place to discuss it. A complete record of who said what. No emails or screenshots required.

The tool
What Groove does
  • 🔑
    Private identity — each person gets a local cryptographic identity. No account, no password, no external service. Your keys stay with you.
  • 📋
    Shared Care Plan viewer — all 10 SCP sections are available as templates. Open any section, view it, edit it, or discuss it.
  • 💬
    Threaded discussions — named conversations tied to specific topics or visits. Reply to individual messages. Pin a specific paragraph from the health record into your message.
  • 📌
    Pin & lift — pin a paragraph from a health record page into a discussion. Lift a message back into the page as a new paragraph.
  • ✏️
    Inline editing — edit any health record entry directly in Groove. Split-pane editor with live markdown preview.
  • 🔒
    Access log — the person of record sees who accessed their workspace and when. Readable only by them.

Non-negotiable rules
How the SCP governs itself
  • 👤
    Person of record controls all access — grants, revocations, and timed duration. No one else can share the plan without their permission.
  • 🏷️
    Every entry by others is marked — Groove's "Entry by someone else" checkbox is non-negotiable. Authorship is always accurate.
  • 📄
    Physician summary is a first-class output — the plan must be exportable as a clean, designed document for the doctor's workflow. (Coming in a future release.)
  • 👁️
    The person sees who accessed their plan — an auditable access log, readable only by the person of record, is a deployment requirement.

These rules are not features. They are the terms on which a person agrees to use a care plan. They are what makes this trustworthy.

First deployment
Superior, Arizona
A small mining town of 2,700. One of the RCN's Neighborhood Development Corporation sites. Community health work led by Chris Casillas.

Pilot design

Care team unit: person + CHW + 1–2 family members. CHWs use phones during home visits — the tool runs in a mobile browser, no installation. Server-mode only (no peer-to-peer sync required for the first deployment). Hosted on RCN infrastructure at the NDC's Wiki Café.

Requirement before go-live: Automatic backend access log must be operational before any real patient data enters the system. Manual access log entries are used for testing only.
