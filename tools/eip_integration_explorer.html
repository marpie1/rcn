Key Insights

  • Governance is the Meta-System - It shapes how all other systems function
  • Finance Controls Access - Financial systems determine access to all necessities
  • Health is Downstream - Health outcomes result from all other systems
  • Municipal Level is Critical - Most direct democracy and most corporate capture
  • State Preemption Blocks Local Control - States limit city authority on critical issues
  • Commons vs. Extractive - Each system has commons-based and extractive institutional forms
  • Ecological Boundaries ≠ Political Boundaries - Watersheds cross jurisdictions
