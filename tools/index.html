RCN Tools and Methods

Free and open source. Everything here runs in a browser — most of it from a file on your own machine, with no server and no account.

Before any of it. Every tool below sits under two models: the Social Action Tetrahedron — persons, ideals, orgs, action — and Beauty, Good, Truth, Economy. All the models below these two must be informed by these, or they will lead to no good.
Methods — how the tools get used together drafted, not yet built5

A tool is a hammer; a method is a process that uses several. These five are written up but do not yet exist as pages you can open — they live in the plan below. A method is a pattern: context, problem, solution, links.

Shelved by the Six Nested Questions — when, where, why, who, how, what — because that is the order a problem is actually worked, and answering what first is the standard failure. A tool can sit on more than one shelf; the Graph Tool answers why in CLD mode and how in OPM and SFD modes.
