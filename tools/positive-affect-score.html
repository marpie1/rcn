What this measures

The ten positive-affect words of the PANAS (Watson, Clark & Tellegen, 1988), rated 1–5. Adding the ten ratings gives a raw score from 10 to 50. It measures felt positive engagement — not mood in general, and not anything about negative feeling unless you also rate the optional NA-10 words.

The timeframe decides what the score means
Right nowState affect — how you feel at this moment. Moves hour to hour. Use this before and after a meeting, or to watch a day. No band is shown, because the band crosswalk is not calibrated for it.
Past weekCloser to a trait reading — your general level lately. Use this for health check-ins. A band 1–4 is shown, using the same provisional crosswalk the Shared Care Plan uses.
Every saved result records which timeframe you used. Do not compare a right-now score against a past-week score.
