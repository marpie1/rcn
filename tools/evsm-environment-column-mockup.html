Environment column — mockup on the existing 11-sphere layout

The eleven spheres and their edges are exactly what the survey and aggregator draw today. Nothing inside the ring changes.

Ten tiles, one per environment coupling from the RCN model, in a column beside the spheres they feed — the same place the RCN diagram puts them. Each tile is colored by the latency input index — the share of respondents who said that coupling is working (Block F, agree 3–4) — and a dashed spoke joins it to its sphere.

working   mixed   blocked   mostly unsure

The number in each tile is the index; the small grey number is the share who left it blank — which is itself a reading of S-9.

Clicking a tile opens the Block F evidence for that coupling in the detail panel, the same way clicking a sphere does today. Tiles are not nodes: they have no go/caution/stop, no edges of their own, no ids in EDGE_LIST. They are drawn from the extra-item block, exactly as the protection items already are.

In the survey tool the same column appears un-colored as navigation: click a tile to answer that Block F item. Two tiles sit on the left because the working groups' inputs and demand feed S-6 and S-3.
