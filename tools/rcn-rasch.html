Labels

Naming these changes every heading. "person / skill" for a badge ledger, "resident / statement" for a survey.

Reading the fit

MNSQ near 1.0 means the data behave the way the model expects.

Above 1.5 — noisy. The item is picking up something other than the thing being measured. This is the one worth investigating.

Below 0.7 — too predictable. Often a duplicate item, or one that just restates another.

Misfit is information. An item that misfits is measuring something else, and finding out what is usually the point.
