Agreement Checker

Write your agreement in plain words. The checker splits it into sentences with one MUST, MAY, or MUST NOT each. It fills in its best guess for every question an agreement should answer, then asks about anything blank, hidden, clashing, or not backed by anyone. Every guess is yours to correct.
