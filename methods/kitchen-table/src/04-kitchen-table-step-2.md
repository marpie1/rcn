# Kitchen Table Step 2

Make a list of anything that comes to mind when you think about that big deal topic.

It is a list, not an essay. Feel free to list anything — an idea, a person, a place, a thing, any thing — that comes to your mind.

Be sure there are at least fifteen items in the list. Do not go further than what can be written on a single sheet of paper.

Take your time. Take a break if you like.

You should end up feeling that a lot of the stuff related to the topic is on that page.
