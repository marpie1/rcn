# Kitchen Table Exercise

An RCN method. One hour, alone, with paper and a pencil. At the end you will have made a clear sparse visual story about something you care about, out of nothing but your own mind and your own experience, and nobody will have helped you make it.

That last part is the method.

## Before you start

- Set aside an hour, and a second hour afterwards. Do it alone.
- Get three or four sheets of paper, a pencil, and an eraser.
- Do not read ahead. The exercise stops working if you do.

## What these pages will not do

They will not help you. There are no examples here, no worked answer, and nothing that tells you whether what you have drawn is any good. That is deliberate, and it is most of the point.

If you find yourself wanting someone to check, notice that and keep going.

Some steps may feel uncomfortable. That is common, it happens to most people, and it is not a sign that you are doing it wrong. The discomfort is part of the material.
