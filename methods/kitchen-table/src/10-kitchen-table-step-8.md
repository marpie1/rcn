# Kitchen Table Step 8

Make sure that each of the labels on the lines is a verb which conveys action or change — typically a word ending in "-ing".

You will probably need to spend some time on this step, and to erase some of the initial labels.

By the way, you can change the labels on the items themselves any time you like.

This is your graph, your drawing, about something that you care about.
