The SODOTO Teaching Graph

A Neo4j graph that turns the teaching relationships hidden inside SODOTO (See One, Do One, Teach One) badges into something you can ask questions of — who taught whom, how a skill spread through the network, who mentors the most, and where every credential came from.

Introduction · August 2026

What it is

Every SODOTO badge already records a small story: a learner earned a skill, a mentor witnessed them, and — at Teach One — they taught it on to a student. Each badge tells one story. The teaching graph gathers all of those stories into a single connected picture, so you can trace the threads between them: the chain of who-taught-whom to any depth, the mentors a skill flows through, the full provenance of any credential.

The badges are the truth; the graph is a lens. Nothing depends on the graph. It is a projection built from the badges — you can delete it and rebuild it from the portfolios at any time, and nothing is lost. Issuing or verifying a credential never touches it.

The questions it answers

A badge answers "does this person hold this skill, and who witnessed it?" The graph answers questions that span the whole network:

  • Teaching lineage — trace a skill back through everyone who taught it: Kerry → Marc → Noah, to any depth.
  • Most active mentors — who has grown the most practitioners?
  • Where a skill entered the network — the roots of a teaching tree.
  • A person's full record — every credential they hold and everyone they taught.
  • Skill coverage — who can do skill X, and how many gates deep.
  • Provenance — which Neighborhood Development Center (NDC) issued a credential, over which gates, with which attestations.

The model at a glance

Five kinds of node and the relationships between them. A Person can be a mentor, a learner, or a student depending on the edge; a Credential ties a learner to a skill and an issuing organization; each completed gate becomes a GateAttempt.

The teaching graph schema. The full list of relationships — including the per-gate ATTESTED, ASSERTED, and WITNESSED edges not drawn here — is in the User Manual.

How it is built

A small script — substrate/sodoto_projector.py — reads the sodoto-badge items out of the FedWiki portfolio pages and upserts the nodes and edges into Neo4j. It is idempotent: keyed on each credential's contractId, so running it again changes nothing. You re-run it whenever you want the graph to reflect new badges. This is the RCN Substrate pattern — one Neo4j graph, and each tool is a lens that reads the layers it needs.

What it is not

  • Not a dependency. The issuer tool and the badge verifier never call it. It runs on the Desktop Substrate, separate from the live WikiCafe system.
  • No debt or value. There is no Debt node. Debt and the three-currency settlement are a separate, standalone system (CfA-dSC — Conversations for Action / dyadic Smart Contracts) — SODOTO provenance stays purely about who taught, held, and issued what.
  • Not the source of truth. If the graph and a badge ever disagree, the badge wins. Rebuild the graph.
