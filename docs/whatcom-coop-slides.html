The founding insight

Most of what creates and preserves your health
happens at home.

The choices you make every day, the people around you, the knowledge you carry, the support you can count on — these determine far more of your health than any clinic visit. Neighborhoods are the true health system. They always have been.

  • Your health record belongs to the clinic — not to you, not in your home
  • Community health workers have the deepest relationships — and the fewest tools
  • When you need medical care, there is no organized picture of your health ready to hand over
  • Rural Whatcom County has never been well-served by a clinic-centered model
  • What has been missing is giving people and their communities everything they need to do what they already do — far better

Part Two

The Tools

My Personal Health Supporter · Open source · Person-owned
My Personal Health Supporter

Three tools. One complete picture.

Tool 1

My Health Picture

Your complete health record — in plain language, owned by you. Every problem, every piece of evidence, every gap in the evidence clearly shown.

Tool 2

My Health Choices

A guided exploration of treatment options. What the evidence says. What the tradeoffs are. Designed to support real informed decision-making.

Tool 3

My Shared Care Plan

A living document your whole care team can see and contribute to. Updated after every interaction. Everyone reads the same record.

My Health Picture — the core tool

The first health record that belongs to you —
not to a clinic, not to a health system

Every health record that existed before this one was owned by an institution. My Health Picture is yours — at home, with you, carried into every conversation with anyone who helps you. It connects to the medical system when you need it. It does not depend on or orbit it.

What it holds

  • Every active problem, in plain language, at its actual level of certainty
  • The evidence behind each diagnosis — and what is still missing
  • Your choices and preferences, updated over time
  • A structured summary ready to hand to any provider, anywhere

What it changes

  • You arrive at every encounter knowing your own health picture
  • Old diagnoses no longer travel silently forward without evidence
  • Your health partner can review it with you at home, before any visit
  • You can share it with family, with a new physician, with anyone you choose
Part Three

The Care Team

Health partners · Physician partners · Self-managing microenterprises
The health partner

The person who has time
to actually listen

What they do

  • Walk through the Health Picture before every physician encounter
  • Gather the structured evidence the physician needs to decide
  • Know when to escalate — and how to hand off efficiently
  • Meet people at home, at community centers, where they are
  • Build trust over time in the community they are from

What they are not

  • A receptionist or data entry clerk
  • A case manager with a checklist and no authority
  • A volunteer doing work the system does not value
  • Someone standing between the person and real care
  • Someone who has to apologize for not being a doctor
The physician partner

Expertise directed where it is most needed

When a case is escalated, the physician receives a structured package — not a stack of old records to interpret in fifteen minutes.

The specific problem
at its level of certainty
→
Evidence gathered
and what is missing
→
CHW observations
what they actually saw
→
One clear question
what do you recommend?

The response goes into the Shared Care Plan — not into a phone call that disappears.

The neighborhood care team

A self-managing microenterprise

Structure

5–15 health partners

A small, self-managing team serving an enrolled neighborhood population. Their own performance record. Their own economics. No management above them — only commitments they have made.

Compensation

Paid for problems resolved

Not for visits logged. The team earns more when more health problems move toward resolution. The system earns when the person gets better — not despite it.

Connection

Contracted physician partners

Physicians are expert partners called on when needed — compensated for quality and responsiveness, their engagement score tracked and rewarded.

The organizing philosophy · 人单合一

人
Rén
Person. Not a role or a resource — a moral agent with dignity and initiative.
单
Dān
Order. A specific, concrete need — not "diabetes management" but this person's open problem, today.
合
Hé
Active merging. The living process of person and need coming together — not yet resolved, but in genuine motion. The care relationship itself.
一
Yī
Achieved oneness. Resolution. The problem actually addressed — not managed, not tracked, but met. What the cooperative is paid for.

The person and the order are in the active process of becoming one. The health partner earns when 一 is achieved — which means the tools, the relationship, and the economics all point toward the same thing: health.

A structural commitment
Open Source · CC 4.0 · Forever

Every tool in this system is open source, licensed Creative Commons 4.0, and will never be for sale.

Anyone can use them, adapt them, deploy them — in Whatcom County, in rural Alaska, in a clinic anywhere in the world — without asking anyone's permission.

The cooperative earns by using the tools well

Not by owning them. The tools belong to the commons. The cooperative is the financial sustainability model.

What happened to Larry Weed's tools

His Problem Knowledge Couplers were trapped in IP disputes after he sold the rights. The vision became inaccessible. That cannot happen here.

Why now — thirty years in the making

We tried this in 1995.
Here is what was missing then.

Whatcom Integrated Delivery System, 1995

  • Organized every medical provider, the hospital, and Blue Cross into one system
  • Built county-wide medical records and a secure healthcare network
  • Washington State mandate for managed care set for July 1995
  • State withdrew the mandate the week before launch
  • The business structure dissolved — the quality work continued

What is different now

  • No state mandate needed — a voluntary cooperative starts with employers who choose to join
  • The tools now exist — My PHS, shared care plans, physician review loops
  • The cooperative model sidesteps institutional permission entirely
  • The same team, thirty years wiser, with the structural conditions finally right

Give people everything they need — and watch what neighborhoods do

Every person has a complete, current picture of their own health — in their hands, at home, owned by them, shareable with anyone they choose.

Every neighborhood has health partners with real tools, real credentials, and real compensation — people from the community, working in the community, for the community.

Every physician's expertise reaches exactly where it is most needed — directed at real decisions, protected from overload, compensated for quality and responsiveness.

The cooperative grows stronger as the community gets healthier — because for the first time, those two things are the same thing.
