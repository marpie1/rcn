# The Two Governing Models

> Beauty is the basis for using all the models. That is why I put it at the top along with the most general model of humans taking action together: the Social Action Tetrahedron. All the models below these two must be informed by these or they will lead to no good. — [[Models for Seeing Systems]]

This is a precondition, not a pathway. Every pathway in [[Six Questions Five Pathways]] runs underneath these two.

## Social Action Tetrahedron

What humans taking action together are made of.

- [[Persons]] who aspire, participate, perform
- [[Ideals]] that motivate, inspire, guide
- [[Orgs]] that prioritize, facilitate, enrol
- [[Action]] that realizes, teaches, engages

The optimum sequence through it is the thick path: a person [[Aspires to]] an ideal, which [[Inspires]] the creation and management of organizations, which then [[Facilitates]] actions, whose result [[Realizes]] the originating ideal. Used as a diagnostic, this becomes [[Walking the Tetrahedron]].

## Beauty Good Truth Economy

What makes the use of a model come to good.

- Beauty — aesthetics, passion
- Good — ethics
- Truth — ecology
- Economy — surplus

BGTE is not decorative and it is not a values statement bolted on the front. In [[Neighborhood Systems Patterns]] it feeds all six of the questions directly, and it feeds Stories, which feed Body-Mood-Language. It is upstream of the whole apparatus.

## What this asks of a method page

Every method carries a line most method documentation never has: what would make running this beautiful, good, true and economical, and what would make it none of those. A method that passes its gates and fails BGTE has not succeeded.
