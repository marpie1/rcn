# Walking the Tetrahedron

**Problem:** Everyone is working hard and nothing is coming of it. Something in the loop between people, ideals, organization and action is broken, and nobody can name which.

**Context:** [[The Two Governing Models]] used as a check. Four questions anyone can ask untrained, and sixteen named places to look. Under an hour, no facilitator needed.

Kind: ontological. Confidence: none yet. It is not one diagnostic among several — it is the governing model applied.

## 1. Do the persons here actually aspire to the ideal?

The relation is [[Aspires to]], from Persons to Ideals.

If no, the quality to look at is [[Aspiration of Persons]]. Nothing downstream can be fixed by better organizing. Check whether the ideal was ever theirs, or was handed to them.

## 2. Does that ideal inspire an organization into being?

The relation is [[Inspires]], from Ideals to Orgs.

If no, look at [[Power of Inspiration of Org]]. An ideal people hold privately, but which has produced no standing arrangement to pursue it, stalls exactly here.

## 3. Does the organization facilitate action?

The relation is [[Facilitates]], from Orgs to Action.

If no, look at [[Quality of Facilitation]]. This is the commonest failure in practice — an organization exists, meets, and produces no action it has made easier.

## 4. Does the action realize the originating ideal?

The relation is [[Realizes]], from Action to Ideals.

If no, look at [[Ideals Realized]]. Work is happening but has drifted from what it was for. The loop never closes, and people burn out without knowing why.

These four are the thick arrows in the diagram: the optimum sequence is literally the heavy path. The remaining eight relations are the repair vocabulary, not the diagnosis.

## 5. Is the action teaching the organization anything?

The relation is [[Teaches]], from Action to Orgs. Outside the optimum loop, checked last, and it decides whether any of this compounds.

Its quality-name is [[Organizational Learning]] — which is [[SODOTO]]'s Teach One at the scale of an institution, sitting inside the frame independently of SODOTO.

## 6. And does the whole of it come to good?

The second half of the crown: Beauty, Good, Truth, Economy.

A tetrahedron whose four arrows all work, in service of something ugly, false, or extractive, is a well-functioning machine pointed the wrong way.
