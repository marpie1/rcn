# Collective Action Assessment

**Problem:** A neighbourhood holds something in common — water, road maintenance, a building, a fund — and nobody knows which parts of managing it are actually at risk.

**Context:** [[WHO Collective Action After Ostrom]] applied to a real commons. The only method in the set whose output is a disagreement.

Kind: diagnostic. Confidence: none yet. Not yet in the SODOTO Skills Registry.

## 1. What do we actually hold in common?

Fill the template's blank nodes with this neighbourhood's real common-pool resources — water, road maintenance, boundary agreements, ethical rules.

Naming them is the first act, and it is where a neighbourhood discovers it has more commons than it thought.

**Gate:** the resource must be genuinely depletable and open to access. If it is neither, this is the wrong model and the pathway should not be entered.

## 2. Where is the risk, node by node?

Colour every node in the model. Red for high risk. Yellow for low risk. Green for little or no risk now. Blue for undecidable risk now.

Blue is doing real work here. It is a legitimate answer, not a failure to answer, and a model with honest blue on it is more useful than one coloured through.

Border widths and arrow widths are adjustable too. The template is meant to be altered, not just filled.

## 3. Do our associates see the same risks?

Have others in the collective action do the exercise separately, before comparing.

**Gate:** assessments must be made independently. A group that colours together produces one opinion wearing several signatures, and that removes the finding.

## 4. Where do we disagree, and especially where is one of us green and another red?

Discuss the differences, especially the instances where there is a green and a red assessment of the same node.

A green-red split on one variable means two people are living in different systems. That is a finding no single assessment could produce.

Print the diagrams and put them on a surface for ongoing conversation. This is a [[Cave Drawing]] in the literal sense — fit for finger pointing — and it is why the method wants paper rather than a screen.

## 5. Which stage are we failing, and what does that route us to?

Read the reds by stage. Concentrated in green forming-variables, the collective action has not properly originated and no amount of management will help. Concentrated in blue, it is a performance problem. Concentrated in red surviving-variables, above all Conflict Resolution, it will not persist however well it currently runs.

**Routes to:** Origin failures go to [[Systemic Problem Solving]]. Performance failures go to [[Sofi VSM]]. Survival failures mean conflict work first and everything else second.
