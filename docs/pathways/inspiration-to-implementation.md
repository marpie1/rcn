# Inspiration to Implementation

**Problem:** Something needs to exist that does not exist, and the group is larger than a few friends — so the fifteen things a small group does intuitively now have to be done on purpose.

**Context:** The long arc, months to years. The [[15 Ps]]. The only pathway that terminates outside itself.

Kind: production. Confidence: none yet.

## 1. What do we notice, and what might it become?

Private — Perception, Possibility, Purpose, Passion. Sensation before language.

These four are easily missed or discounted precisely because they are not yet in words, and a group that skips them builds something nobody wants.

## 2. Who will commit, who will we bring in, and what do we decide?

Public — Promise, Political Action, Policy.

Promise is an embodied commitment, even if only to oneself. Political action is repeating the first five in each other person. Policy is the organization deciding to act.

**Gate:** no program without a promise. Planning entered before anyone has committed produces plans that nobody owns.

## 3. How will the work be organized and resourced?

Planning — Program, Project, Process.

Programs carry the resources, projects carry the sub-plans, and processes create a reliable sequence for producing the goods or services.

## 4. Does it get made, bought, delivered and kept safe?

Production — Product, Persuade, Purchase, Provide, Protect.

Protect includes maintenance, disposal and recycling, and the liabilities of failure — which is where most well-meant neighbourhood projects quietly stop.

## 5. What is universal here that someone else could use?

Posterity — [[Pattern Writing]]. Abstract what is general and hand it on.

This step is coloured orange for danger: if we do not share the recipes, innovations will not spread fast enough to make the change to a low energy world before it is too late.

**Closes the whole system:** this is the move from model to pattern described in [[Models and Patterns]]. It writes a new page onto the shelf, so Posterity is not the end of this pathway — it is the input to everyone else's [[Systemic Problem Solving]].
