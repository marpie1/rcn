# Interrogating A System

Kerry Turner's way of building a causal loop diagram with someone. It is the second door into the same room as [[Eleven Steps]], and nothing in it is top down.

## The referent is the learner's before any notation appears

She opens by asking what you want to get out of the session. Chris's answer was a board retreat in a fortnight.

The homework came first: a set of questions honed over time, answered in his own prose, in his own document, about his own situation. So when variables finally arrive they are extracted from sentences he had already written about something he already cared about. Authorship is established upstream of the technique rather than asserted during it.

The questions nest. For any answer, what enables this, and what might hinder it. One of them asks what the land wants from us, which Chris first answered as though it had asked what we want from the land.

## Systems before notation

Parts against connections. Emergent properties. The aeroplane built from the finest parts in the world and fastened together with string. It is easy to see the parts and very difficult to see the connections, and the connections are what make the difference.

Then Fred and the dominoes, which carries most of the load. You are inside the system and cannot see it. The effect is the opposite of the intention. The signature of not seeing the system is asking who did that to me. Hidden assumptions surface, such as the dominoes being heavy. Set up physically with children it behaves differently every time, which gives the real conclusion: whenever we intervene we never really know what will happen, so we are always running an experiment, and should make it safe and watch the result. Widening the perspective opens up choices — children say turn sideways and push both, and adults mostly do not.

## Variables from the room you are sitting in

Not a definition. Give me some examples of variables in your room. Temperature, light, the number of chairs. Then a noticing: you named temperature first, and that is something you cannot see. Then inward — variables about you. Heart rate, energy, enthusiasm, attention. Then the strength of the connection between us on this call right now, which is also a variable.

Same move as the first two of the [[Eleven Steps]]. Begin with what is present and yours.

## Two relationships, read aloud

Supporting, same direction. Opposing, opposite direction. All other things held equal. And every arrow gets spoken as English: effort affects results in the same direction.

Reading aloud appears in both methods independently, which is stronger evidence that it is load bearing than either one alone.

## The interrogation engine

- You said effort affects results. Is there anything else?
- How? — to surface an intermediate variable, or to conclude the relationship is direct.
- What affects effort? What might help? What might hinder?
- What does this affect? Why does it matter here? What are the consequences of more or less of it?
- What is it about this that matters here?
- Addiction to what?

And the rule that unlocks soft variables: forget about measurement for now, this is not about whether you can measure it, it is about whether it matters.

## Loops emerge rather than being hunted

Kerry does not go looking for feedback. She looks after the integrity of the variables and the relationships, and the feedback emerges — and people are regularly surprised by how much of it there is.

To classify a loop, count the opposing relationships around it. Zero or an even number is reinforcing. An odd number is balancing.

Delays get marked, because they cause instability and most traps around them come from not having noticed them.

Structure drives behaviour rather than the words. Change chickens and eggs to humans and babies and the structure is unchanged, which is why understanding one system transfers to another.

## Never finished, and never the point

The diagram is never complete. Good enough is when the people in the room have confidence that it reflects their own understanding and that they have genuinely taken in perspectives they have not lived. The level of detail is set by the interventions you intend to make.

Then you use it to design an experiment, run it, take the feedback from the real system, and feed that back into the diagram. The purpose is always learning.

An agreed map matters because people who share one act in a coordinated way, where people holding different private models cannot.

## Do not hand over a straw man

Marc's position in that session, and Kerry's agreement with it: the board should build their own diagram even through the discomfort of not knowing how, because if they become dependent on work somebody else did, the block under their wheels is still there. They also then need you less, which is the point.

## The tool ladder

Paper, pencil and eraser. Then sticky notes with one arrow colour for supporting and another for opposing. Then software that finds every loop in the model and unravels the spaghetti into a driver tree or a consequences tree. Then stock and flow, and then a calibrated model that answers how much rather than which way.

Each rung is entered because the previous one broke down, not because it was next on a list.

## Diagram and prose convert both ways

A good causal loop diagram converts into good English, and doing so strips the fluff. Kerry used to draw the diagram, write the prose from it, and throw the diagram away.

And running it the other way, with Ward Cunningham, turning personal stories into diagrams revealed that a single story's structure contains many potential stories — the ones that were not chosen are still in the graph. Which is how a syndicate keeps producing new ones from the same shape.

## The two doors

Both methods start from the person's live concern, in their own words, with no answer key, reading the relation aloud, letting the constraint do the correcting, and iterating.

[[Eleven Steps]] is solo, silent, defers abstraction entirely, and is instrumented for feeling. It produces authorship and has no intervention step.

This one is dialogic, question driven, abstract from the start, and instrumented for consequence. It produces rigour and has no reflection step.

So the sequence is eleven steps to make it yours, these questions to make it true, an experiment to make it matter, and back to the diagram.

## Why a decade of this in British schools mostly did not take

Not the method. The method is authored throughout. The institution is the constraint.

This way of working needs four things, and a timetable supplies none of them. A live concern the learner actually has, which is not in the syllabus. A real intervention at the end, because the loop only closes when you act and bring the feedback back, and an unclosed diagram is decoration however well authored. Unhurried time with a practised interlocutor, since ninety minutes one to one produced a four variable loop. And permission to be uncomfortable without being marked.

The finding is worse and more useful than blaming anyone: an institution can take a fully authored method and convert it into content purely by its structure, with nobody intending it. See [[Credit Ladder]] for why the NDC school can supply those four conditions and a school cannot.
