# RCN Tools In The School

Where each RCN tool lands, arranged by the five planes in [[NDC School Superior]] rather than as an inventory.

## Commons — what is out there

- RCN Map is the spine. Geology, water, land status across Forest Service, State Trust, private and tribal, historic structures, oral history pins, plant occurrences from the arboretum, heat and shade, the projected subsidence footprint. Every student adds layers, and over a decade this becomes the town's most valuable asset and belongs to the town.
- Issue Polygon Map holds parcels, ownership, the land exchange boundary, and the geographic version of the left and right polygons.
- RCN Timeline runs from the 1870s to now, boom and bust, with Allen relations so that during the strike and after the strike become arguable claims rather than loose phrasing. Students verify every date against the town's own records, and that verification is the history course.
- The substrate and its Layer 1 static projections give one graph and many lenses with no server. Each site is its own substrate, sealed and verifiable offline, which is the right architecture for a town with patchy connectivity.

## Practices — the repeated moves

- Graph tool in causal loop mode, from about age seven. Two arrows and a story: more of this, more of that; more of this, less of that. Reinforcing is a snowball and balancing is a thermostat. Starter loops come from town life — more mine jobs, more people, more stores, more reason to stay; fewer people, a smaller school, fewer reasons to stay; more pumping, a lower water table, deeper wells, higher cost, less pumping.
- Stock and flow mode once stock and flow can be held apart, around eleven or twelve. Bathtub first, then real bathtubs: the town water tank, the creek, enrolment, the stock of rules. Export to XMILE, simulate, be wrong, revise.
- Vester's impact matrix on a dozen town variables, sorting levers from indicators. A one-day exercise that reorganises how a room thinks.
- Ecology, institutions and politics mode for what a law actually does. A statute lands in politics, deforms an institution, and the institution's operations hit the ecology, and the interesting arrows are the slow return paths. Each side of a dispute builds its own full picture and only what both put there goes in the centre. The centre starts small and grows slowly, and the point is that the disagreement becomes precise and stays legitimate instead of being papered over.
- Neighbourhood risk management and Tripod Beta after any real incident — a flood, a mine accident, a fire, a near miss at the crossing. Real incident analysis, done properly, by teenagers.
- Object process methodology for structure without arithmetic, with the Odum coupling for value flows.
- Odum money counterflow for copper out and dollars in, and then the second circuit: where those dollars go inside town and how fast they leak. A measurable number and a good project.
- Wardley mapping with rent band analysis for older students — where the town's economy sits on the evolution axis, what is being rented from it, and what is genuinely commodity.
- Linkage of processes for the school as a system, drawn by the people inside it.
- Statistical process control on the creek gauge, the rain gauge, the well levels, the air monitor, clinic visits, and the quality of the town's conversations. A town that keeps its own control charts argues differently.
- The Rasch tool for the ruler and the Wright map, which is the transcript. See [[Curriculum Is A Record]].
- Bias Checker, run on the school's own journalism before it goes out.
- A3 as every project's one-page discipline.
- Graph Composer for the same picture at family, schema, variable and instance level, which is how a seven-year-old and a seventeen-year-old work on the same drawing at different grain.

## Record — what persists

- See one do one teach one is the journal of the learning journey. A self-custodied identifier minted on the student's own device and never held by the school. Every badge is a signed credential naming the learner, the mentor, the artifact and the date, producing a transparent chain of who learned what from whom that the student carries out the door and the school cannot revoke or lose. In a town where the district might close, that portability is not a nicety.
- FedWiki holds everything, forkable — curricula, drawings, oral histories, the rule graph, the constitutions.
- The rcngraph, rcntable and rcnoutliner plugins keep diagrams editable in place rather than flattened into pictures. A drawing nobody can edit has already stopped being a campfire object.
- MORE Outliner for thinking in structure before it becomes a diagram.
- Shared Care Plan and Groove for the health strand, and for a town with no hospital.
- Wiki Café for hosting, and for the pattern of a site per person.

## Arenas and Constitution

Arenas are mostly not tools. Outside is the default, rooms are named for what is made in them, and the fire circle needs food and a talking piece more than software. The constitutional plane needs only the wiki's page history, which is what makes an amendment visible, and the one-page template in [[Constitution Of The Gap Desk]].
