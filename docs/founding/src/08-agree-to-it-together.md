# Agree To It Together

Step 8, a gate. Everyone at the founding table agrees, and the record says how they agreed.

## Why everyone, this once

The founding agreement can't be made by a vote, because no voting rule exists until it is adopted. So the first adoption is by everyone at the table. After that, the group changes its rules the way the rules themselves say.

## What gets written

- The date.
- Who agreed, by name.
- How they agreed: everyone present said yes.
- Who was invited and absent, and how they can add their agreement later.
- The ethical check's first assessment, if it has one.

## At Elm Street

At the first meeting all fourteen founders say yes. Their names go on the page with the date. The two who couldn't come sign the following week.

## In the RCN Constitution

The Ratification section reads "Date: _________ Signatures: _________". The Constitution has not yet recorded its own adoption. Filling in that line, and saying how it was agreed, is a small act that turns a draft into the network's founding rules.

For an NDC: plan the adoption meeting so the people with least voice are present, not just informed.

## Ostrom's words

The founding act happens at the constitutional level, made in the unwritten way the metaconstitutional level allows, because no written way of deciding exists yet.
