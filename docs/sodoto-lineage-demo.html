A badge that knows where its skill came from

Marc Pierson and Claude Opus 5.5 · September 2026

Today a SODOTO badge names the mentor who witnessed each gate, and stops there. In v0.6 each mentor also carries a pointer to the credential that qualified them: a hash of that credential's signed record, and where to find it. Follow the pointers and you reach the NDC's founding cohort. Check each hash and signature on the way, and you know the whole chain is genuine.

Click Verify Credential on Sam's badge, then Show lineage.

What is real here: every key, signature and hash. The page made fresh Ed25519 keys for a fictional NDC and seven fictional people, signed their credentials and a founding statement, and your browser checks all of it when you click. What is simulated: the network. Fetching each person's portfolio page reads from data inside this page, with a short delay so you can watch the walk.

What changed from today's badge

  • The label adds lineage recorded beside mutually attested.
  • The verify panel gains a Lineage row: how many generations back to the founding cohort, and a Show lineage button.
  • The chain view lists each generation oldest first. Every link gets five checks: hash (the pointer matches the record), signed (the NDC's signature is valid), holder (the credential belongs to the mentor who signed this person's gate), skill (same skill), and in time (the mentor held it before they taught it). Hover a check to see what it means.
  • Went on to lists who this person taught. The Teach One student comes from the badge itself. Anyone who later earned the skill with this person as mentor is found through the NDC's ledger, then checked the same way. The ledger can only help find people; it can never add someone whose own credential doesn't point back.
  • Credentials issued before v0.6 have no pointer. The walk stops at them and says so, rather than guessing.

The design, its checks and the open decisions are in docs/sodoto-lineage-spec.md.
