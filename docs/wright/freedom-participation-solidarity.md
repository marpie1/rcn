# Freedom Participation Solidarity

On the last page of the annotated paper there is a small red drawing: three circles marked **F**, **P** and **S**, with **Team** written above, **Pack** at the lower left and **Chain** at the lower right. Freedom, Participation, Solidarity.

Marc has told this story elsewhere. Asked in a facilitated circle to say what he cared most about, he heard himself say "freedom and solidarity" and had no idea what he meant. Years of puzzling produced the answer that the load-bearing word was the conjunction, and that **AND = PARTICIPATION** — see [[Freedom AND Solidarity]] and [[Freedom AND Solidarity as ReLocalize Creativity]].

That frame gives three tests. Solidarity without participation is domination. Freedom without participation is isolation. Freedom and solidarity together require participation in all decision-making that affects others.

## Wright supplies the mechanics

The correspondence is close, and one leg of it is Wright's own word rather than an interpretation laid over him.

A **Pack** is Freedom. Members try different things, no agreement is required of anyone, and a member who fails costs the Pack nothing. Maximum individual latitude, structurally guaranteed.

A **Chain** is Solidarity. Wright's sentence is "A Chain relies on solidarity. It works when everyone is in lock step, but it fails when any member stumbles." Mutual reliance, and mutual exposure to one another's failure.

A **Team** is what participation makes possible — the state in which freedom and solidarity are held at once, which is why it sits at the top of the drawing rather than beside the other two.

## The three pathologies

What makes this more than a tidy analogy is that Wright independently produces the failure modes, including one the frame does not name.

**Freedom without participation is isolation.** In Wright's terms, a Pack that never converges. When the Chain disintegrates, he writes, "the former members are forced to fend for themselves." Endless divergence, nothing ever built.

**Solidarity without participation is domination.** A Chain that nobody chose to be in. Lock step imposed rather than undertaken — and Wright's defensive branch, "circle the wagons, regress to a previous Chain in desperate hope of weathering the storm," which he says destroys the Team when it fails.

**Consensus without participation is a mob.** This is the one the frame does not name and Wright does: "A mob is a Team that chooses the wrong solution to a difficult social problem." A Team that skipped the Pack phase is domination wearing consensus clothing, and the arithmetic says it will be the worst of the three available options.

## What that makes of the sequence

Pack → Chain → Team stops being a maturity model that groups climb once and becomes **the path participation has to walk for freedom and solidarity to be co-possible**. Each shortcut has a name and a named pathology. You cannot start at Team; a Team assembled without the Pack phase is a mob by construction.

This is also why the Quaker Friends Meeting is the right reference. What happens there, in Marc's description, is "not agreement or consensus — it is global understanding," Follett's whole in every part. In Wright's terms that is the requirement to run the Pack long enough, and honestly enough, before anyone is entitled to the Team.

## Cave drawings are the participation mechanism

The ReLocalize Creativity page names cave drawings as the instrument of synthesis: explicit, evolving models that make everyone's mental models visible, comparable and discussable, built not to analyse a system but to get the whole into every part.

Read against Wright, that is a precise functional claim. **The cave drawing is what lets a Pack's scattered findings become a Chain and then a Team without anyone being coerced into agreement.** It is how the transition happens participatively rather than by authority — which is to say, it is the thing that keeps solidarity from becoming domination on the way up.

The Ethical Regulator Theorem sits in the same place. Ashby's requirement that every good regulator be a model of the system could just as easily license technocratic control — solidarity without participation. It becomes participatory self-regulation only when the modelling itself is participatory. See [[Requisite Variety]] for the variety-matching half of that argument, of which Wright's `(B̄ − D)` is the scalar form.
