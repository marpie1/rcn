# Civil Society Back In The Game

ReLocalize Creativity exists to put civil society back in the game against remote government and remote landlords. Wright's compositions turn out to describe that situation exactly, and to say something specific about what "back in the game" would require.

## Remote authority is a Chain whose deciding link is elsewhere

A decision about a locale that is made outside it arrives through a chain. Tenant to property manager to management company to the fund that owns the building to the capital behind the fund. Neighborhood need to city department to state agency to federal rule to appropriation. Five links, six, more.

Two things follow from the arithmetic, and they are the whole problem.

The first is ordinary: `BC = B̄ − ln N`. The chain is weak, and it gets weaker with every link. Long remote chains fail often, and they fail silently, because a break anywhere upstream looks from the locale like nothing happening.

The second is the one that matters. **The links that decide are not local, so the people in the locale cannot re-wire the chain.** Everything in [[The Group Of Five]] depends on a group being able to change its own composition when the problem changes. A group that cannot re-wire has exactly one mode available to it — the one it was placed in by somebody else — and that mode is a chain, and it is at the far weak end of it.

This is what "out of the game" means in composition terms. Not that civil society is weak. That it has been composed into an arrangement it does not control, in the mode with the worst arithmetic, at the end furthest from the decision.

## Which makes it domination, mechanically

[[Freedom Participation Solidarity]] gives the test: solidarity without participation is domination. A chain is solidarity — mutual reliance, shared exposure to one another's failure. A chain nobody in the locale chose to be in, and nobody in the locale can leave or alter, is solidarity without participation.

That is not a rhetorical flourish laid over the arithmetic. It is the same statement twice. Wright's Chain is the mode that requires everyone to hold and punishes any deviation. Imposed from outside, that description *is* domination, and no additional political premise is needed to get there.

## Why adding a local coordinator does not fix it

The standard remedy is a liaison, a navigator, a community engagement officer, a coordinating role sitting between the locale and the remote apparatus.

`−ln 5 = −1.61`. `−ln 6 = −1.79`. **The coordinator is another link.** A role introduced to strengthen the connection lengthens the thing it was hired to strengthen, and leaves the deciding link exactly as far away as it was.

The same applies to consultation, to advisory boards, and to any arrangement that adds a station on the path without moving where the decision is made. If the deciding link stays remote, the chain has grown.

## What relocalizing means arithmetically

There are only two moves the arithmetic permits, and "ReLocalize" names both.

**Shorten the chain by bringing the deciding link inside the locale.** Not consultation on a decision made elsewhere — the decision. Every link removed is worth `ln N − ln(N−1)`, and the links worth removing most are the ones between the locale and the decision.

**Convert chain segments into local packs.** Where a single remote route now exists, put several local attempts, first success wins. This is the move that changes the sign: `−ln N` becomes `+ln(N·W)`. And it is the only composition whose strength does not depend on knowing `D`, which matters because nobody remote has ever calibrated the difficulty of a local problem correctly.

## The diversity that makes this work is already there

The obvious objection is that a handful of neighbours cannot match a professional apparatus. Wright's arithmetic says otherwise, and [[What Wright Assumed]] carries the numbers.

Pack strength is `ln(N_eff · W)`, where `N_eff` deflates with `ρ`, the correlation in approach. Five people trained on one protocol, supervised by one person, working from one evidence base sit around `ρ = 0.7` — worth about **+0.27** logits between them, barely more than one person acting alone. Five neighbours who share a locale but not a training, a livelihood, a network or a class position sit near `ρ = 0.1` — worth about **+1.27**, and more once `W` is counted, because heterogeneity raises pack strength directly.

The local group wins by roughly a logit, for precisely the reason the professional team was assembled: uniformity. Shared training makes carbon copies, and a pack of carbon copies is a pack of one.

This is not an argument that expertise is worthless. It is an argument about *where expertise should sit in the composition* — in the standard processes a pack has already discovered and written down (see [[Discovering Standard Processes]]), not in the membership of the pack that has to face what is not yet standard.

## What "back in the game" would look like

A locale where the deciding link is local. A standing group of about five, diverse by circumstance rather than by recruitment policy, that can name which mode it is in and change it. Packs run at problems nobody has solved. Chains kept short and local. Teams convened only on work already made easy, and only by people who were in the pack.

That group is not weaker than the remote apparatus it replaces. On hard problems — the only ones that matter here — it is stronger, and Wright wrote down why in 1995.
