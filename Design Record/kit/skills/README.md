# Skill triples — the template

Every pattern in the Kit gets three SODOTO credentials, Basic, Intermediate, and Advanced, and each credential is earned through SODOTO's three gates: See One, Do One, Teach One. So each pattern has three pages here, and each page has the same parts. The e-VSM triple (`evsm-basic.md`, `evsm-intermediate.md`, `evsm-advanced.md`) is the template; write the others to its form. Pages are pipeline markdown, one paragraph per item, ready for the fedwiki-page skill to turn into FedWiki pages a learner forks into their own site.

The parts of a skill page:

**What this credential says about you.** The ladder line from the pattern, in the second person: what a person who holds it can do.

**Before you start.** The prerequisite credentials, if any, and the one or two things to read or watch first, pointed at where they live (`tool-index.md`).

**Gate 1 — See One.** What to watch a mentor do, in real work and not a demonstration staged for you; what to look for; what to write in the See One narrative page afterward. The mentor asserts *showed*; the learner asserts *understood*.

**Gate 2 — Do One.** The real task the learner does with the mentor watching; the artifact it produces; what the mentor is looking for to confirm the gate, stated so that both know when it is met; what to write in the Do One page. The mentor asserts *satisfied* or *not satisfied* (and another attempt is scheduled, with no penalty); the learner asserts *has done*.

**Gate 3 — Teach One.** What the learner's student does at their own See One and Do One under the learner's teaching, with the mentor watching the teaching; the student's Do One page is the evidence. The mentor asserts *satisfied*; the learner asserts *has taught*.

**Where this leads.** The next credential up, and which Field Guide phases the skill is used in.

Two rules from SODOTO itself. Every gate happens in real work with a real person witnessing; nothing here is a test. And the narrative pages are the record: a badge is issued by the NDC coordinator only after the pages exist and the assertions are recorded, and it is written into the learner's own portfolio, signed by the NDC, verifiable by anyone.
