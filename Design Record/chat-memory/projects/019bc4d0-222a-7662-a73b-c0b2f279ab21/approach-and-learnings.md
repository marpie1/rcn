## Key learnings & principles
- [stated] Traditional corporate strategic tools need fundamental adaptation for community contexts rather than direct application
- [stated] "Persistent syntegration" addresses a key limitation of compressed business implementations — the tendency to miss important conversations and create undue pressure
- [stated] Community organizing requires different timeframes, processes, and outcomes than corporate strategic planning
- [stated] The innovative approach maintains the structural benefits of syntegration while adapting the pace and focus for grassroots community needs

## Approach & patterns
- [stated] Methodology combines systems thinking with community organizing principles
- [stated] Frameworks are designed to serve substantive community topics and organizational processes simultaneously
- [stated] Uses detailed visual mapping (graphviz diagrams) to structure complex community dialogue systems
- [stated] Works collaboratively with other experts in structured dialogue methods
- [stated] Seeks synergies between different approaches and timeframes where various tools can complement each other in community development work
