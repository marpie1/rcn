- [stated] Marc is developing community organizing methodologies that adapt corporate strategic dialogue tools for grassroots community contexts
- [stated] The work is titled "Campfire Conversations and Cave Drawings"
- [stated] Draws heavily from David Bohm's insights on dialogue
- [stated] Core objective: creating viable community systems that address both substantive community needs and organizational processes
- [stated] Aim is to move beyond traditional business-focused implementations toward community viability and empowerment
- [stated] Marc works with structured dialogue methods including Syntegration, SDD/ISM, and Open Space Technology
- [stated] Collaborating with Peter Tuddenham to help others develop modeling skills in these areas
- [stated] The approach is a fundamental redesign of existing corporate methods, not a simple application of them

## Current state
- [stated] Marc has developed a detailed "slow syntegration" (also called "persistent syntegration") framework designed to avoid the pressures and missing conversations of compressed 3-day business implementations
- [stated] The current design features 12 topic nodes
- [stated] 7 metasystemic topics: Education, Local Economy, Food, Land & Ecology, Care, Health, Security
- [stated] 5 metastructure topics: Collaboration, Coordination, Cooperation, Codesign, Co-Identification
- [stated] The framework incorporates deliberately chosen conversation styles: Six Thinking Hats, Navigating, Conversation for Action, Dialogue, Discussion, and various implementation phases
