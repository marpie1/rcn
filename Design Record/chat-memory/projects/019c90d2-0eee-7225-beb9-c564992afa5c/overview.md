## Purpose and mission

- [stated] Marc is developing ReLocalize Creativity, a neighborhood-scale organizing and institutional design enterprise
- [stated] Mission: "One Idea (See the systems you are in), One Million Neighborhoods."
- [stated] Core goal is enabling every neighborhood on earth to become capable of self-organization and collective action, rather than depending on remote solutions that displace local capacity
- [stated] All work is open source under CC 4.0 licensing, anticipating eventual commercial uptake while maintaining commons-based principles
- [stated] Marc works as a facilitator and community organizer with hands-on experience applying Marshall Ganz's public narrative methodology and the Viable System Model (VSM) in real-world settings
- [stated] He collaborates with a network of neighborhoods and neighborhood development centers

## Frameworks and tools in development (current state)

- [stated] SOFI-VSM diagrams — serve dual roles as both assessment tools (revealing missing conversations) and design instruments for experiments; the tool and the practice are understood to be the same thing
- [stated] EIP (Ecological-Institutional-Political) Stage sketch — a diagnostic storyboard mapping institutions within physical/ecological and sociopolitical environments, with accountability ideally flowing toward ecological health rather than upward to distant hierarchies
- [stated] 26 Essential Functions framework — organized around five hubs (Social, Infrastructure, Safety, Energy, Health), enabling communities to ask where each function currently lives institutionally, at what scale, and with what motivation and accountability structures
- [stated] SODOTO (See One, Do One, Teach One) — an apprenticeship certification methodology developed after finding traditional curricula ineffective, with roughly 1-in-5 success rates, designed to scale to one million neighborhoods
- [stated] Campfire Conversations — recurring conversational infrastructure as the relational foundation for self-organization
- [stated] Cave Drawings — persistent visual artifacts (like SOFI-VSM diagrams) that make shared understanding visible and discussable; Marc prefers "discussable" over "manipulable", preserving agency with people in conversation rather than external experts
- [stated] Marc has applied Ganz's three-story organizing methodology (Story of Self, Story of Us, Story of Now) in real settings, including a board retreat for a locally controlled health insurance cooperative in Oregon
- [stated] He is exploring how to extend the three-story model to neighborhoods through community-led recording and storytelling

## Publication practice

- [stated] Marc publishes daily conversational essays called "Mornings with Marc and Claude" on a FedWiki site, modeling the format after Tuesdays with Morrie
- [stated] He treats joint thinking-writing with Claude as a generative practice that produces greater depth and language fitness than working alone — a collaborative thinking-writing practice, not just a query tool; the joint process itself is part of the methodology
- [stated] Marc regularly requests verbatim markdown transcripts of conversations for publication and archiving
