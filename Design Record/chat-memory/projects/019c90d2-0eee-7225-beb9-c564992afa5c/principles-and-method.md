## Key learnings and principles

- [stated] Remote solutions displace local capacity: outside interventions, regardless of good intentions, can function as "absentee landlords" — creating dependency and redirecting neighborhood attention away from locally-generated solutions
- [stated] The problem generates the gathering: successful neighborhood transformation starts with specific, complex problems residents are already experiencing and meeting about; effective leaders practice diagnostic listening to recognize what's already trying to happen
- [stated] Agency must remain with people in conversation: artifacts and tools should be discussable, not manipulable by experts — the framing matters, reflecting a core value about where authority lives
- [stated] Personal choice and negotiation opportunities are essential principles that must be continuously reflected upon; their success or failure becomes the source of local culture and trust necessary for collective action
- [stated] Founding team dynamics matter: the most successful transformational efforts emerge from small founding teams (2–3 people) who develop approaches privately before going public, combining initiating and sustaining personality types; adding people after going public is problematic because newcomers project structure that constrains participation
- [stated] Vulnerability, not egocentrism, is the heart of Story of Self: honest, emotionally exposed delivery is the mechanism by which others recognize shared humanity and become willing to commit to collective action
- [stated] Failed attempts at imposed leadership are valuable data: they help neighborhoods learn their own character through rejecting what doesn't fit
- [stated] Organizing for vs. organizing against: Ganz's narrative framework is positioned as a mechanism for sustaining generative "for" energy by grounding motivation in personal values rather than opposition
- [stated] All economic value is produced at the first level (collaborate) where actual work happens — a foundational insight from the VSM five-level framework

## Approach and design patterns

- [stated] Marc uses a capability stack framing with four layers: speech act practice, campfire infrastructure, cave drawing artifacts, and coupling mechanisms
- [stated] He adapts the Haier Group's RenDanHeYi model for neighborhood-scale entrepreneurialism — embedding speech acts into organizational structure and directly coupling micro-enterprise teams to the users they serve
- [stated] Diagrams are structured using variables rather than concepts to support causal loop diagrams (CLD) in white spaces, requiring participants to develop CLD literacy
