- [stated] Cohorts of young neighborhood actionists move through RCN network spending weeks together in each place
- [stated] Initial sites: Superior AZ ([[chris-casillas]] at The Fledge), Lansing MI ([[jerry]] at Leo's), Whatcom County WA
- [stated] Networked version of Myles Horton's Highlander Folk School (later Highlander Research and Education Center)
- [stated] Participants learn as much from one another as from the places and people they visit
- [stated] Serves as the generative response for neighborhoods not yet ready (missing founded commons or small groups) — introduce their actionists to networks with vibrant founded commons rather than force readiness
- [stated] "Actionist" is [[chris-casillas]]'s term, distinct from "activist" — emphasizes doing over political-activism connotation
